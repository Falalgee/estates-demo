About Falalu Estates

An independent agent built on one idea: know your patch properly, then treat every instruction like it's your own street.

Built on the streets we sell

Falalu Estates was founded by people who live in the patch they cover — Wimbledon school runs, Barnes riverside weekends, Putney rush hours. That's the whole difference. When we value your home, we've been down your road. When we negotiate for you, we know what the corner mews sold for and why.

We stay deliberately independent and deliberately local: no branch targets, no corporate scripts, no handed-off vendors. One named partner runs your sale or let from photos to keys.

It's a slower way to grow and a better way to sell — 4.9★ across our reviews and most of our business arriving by referral says the trade is worth it.
